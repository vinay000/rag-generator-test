# Classic Italian Lasagna Bolognese

## Authentic Ragù Bolognese
- 500g Ground Beef (80/20 mix)
- 300g Ground Pork
- 100g Pancetta, finely diced
- 1 medium Onion, 2 stalks Celery, 1 large Carrot (finely minced soffritto)
- 2 tablespoons Tomato Paste (concentrato)
- 200ml Dry White Wine
- 200ml Whole Milk
- 800g San Marzano Crushed Tomatoes
- Extra Virgin Olive Oil, Salt, Black Pepper, Fresh Nutmeg

### Preparation of Ragù
1. Sauté the pancetta in a heavy Dutch oven until fat renders. Add minced vegetables and cook gently for 10 minutes until soft.
2. Add the ground beef and pork, breaking up meat with a wooden spoon until browned.
3. Pour in the white wine and simmer until alcohol evaporates completely.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and crushed tomatoes. Season with salt, freshly cracked pepper, and a pinch of grated nutmeg.
5. Lower heat to minimum and simmer gently uncovered for 3 to 4 hours, stirring occasionally and adding warm whole milk in batches to keep meat tender.

## Silky Béchamel Sauce (Besciamella)
- 70g Unsalted Butter
- 70g All-Purpose Flour
- 1 Litre Whole Milk (warmed)
- Freshly grated Nutmeg (1/4 tsp), Salt and White Pepper to taste

Melt butter over medium heat, whisk in flour and cook roux for 2 minutes. Gradually whisk in warm milk to prevent lumps. Simmer on low heat until sauce coats the back of a spoon (about 8 minutes). Season with salt, white pepper, and nutmeg.

## Assembly and Baking
1. Layer in a 9x13-inch ceramic baking dish: thin layer of Béchamel on the bottom, lasagna sheets, rich Ragù Bolognese, Béchamel sauce, and freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese (24-month aged).
2. Repeat for 5 full layers, finishing the top with generous Béchamel and Parmigiano-Reggiano.
3. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 35 minutes covered with foil, then uncover and broil for 5 minutes until bubbly with golden-brown crust.
4. Rest for 20 minutes before slicing to allow layers to set cleanly.
