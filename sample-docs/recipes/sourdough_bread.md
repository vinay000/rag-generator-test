# Artisan Sourdough Bread Recipe

## Ingredients & Baker's Percentages
- Bread Flour (12.7% protein): 450g (90%)
- Whole Wheat Flour: 50g (10%)
- Filtered Water (room temperature 24°C / 75°F): 375g (75% hydration)
- Active Sourdough Starter (100% hydration fed 4 hours prior): 100g (20%)
- Fine Sea Salt: 10g (2%)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Autolyse
Mix the flours and 350g of water in a large glass bowl until no dry flour remains. Cover and let rest for 45 minutes to allow gluten to develop naturally.

### 2. Inoculation and Bulk Fermentation
Add the 100g of active sourdough starter, the remaining 25g of water, and 10g of salt. Dimple and fold the dough to fully incorporate the starter and salt.
Perform 4 sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 minutes apart over the first 2 hours of bulk fermentation.
Allow the dough to ferment at 26°C (78°F) until it has increased in volume by 50% and shows bubbles on the surface (approximately 4 to 5 hours total).

### 3. Shaping and Cold Retardation
Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Preshape into a round boule and bench rest for 20 minutes.
Final shape into an oval batard with good surface tension. Place into an oval banneton dusted with rice flour.
Transfer to the refrigerator at 3°C to 4°C (38°F) for cold retardation for 12 to 16 hours.

### 4. Baking in Dutch Oven
Preheat your Dutch oven inside the oven to 230°C (450°F) for 45 minutes.
Invert dough onto parchment paper, score diagonally with a razor blade at a 30-degree angle.
Bake with the lid on for 20 minutes (retaining steam for oven spring), then remove the lid and bake for another 22 minutes at 220°C (425°F) until the crust turns deep mahogany.
Let the bread cool completely on a wire rack for at least 2 hours before slicing.
